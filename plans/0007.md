# 0007 — Auto-connect via gossipsub (connected peers only)

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Status** | superseded (cross-NAT) |
| **SPEC** | [§9 Signaling](../SPEC.md) |
| **Follow-up** | [0008](0008.md) relay + rendezvous |

## Summary

Restore a minimal libp2p gossipsub overlay. When a contact is a **connected libp2p peer**, opening their chat auto-exchanges WebRTC SDP over `vibe/signal/<conversation_id>`. Manual `vibe://connect` links remain as an advanced fallback.

Cross-NAT discovery and dial is handled by [0008](0008.md) (circuit relay + rendezvous). Direct TCP + `dialAddrs` from invite QR remains a LAN optimization.

## Changes

- Slim [`network.rs`](../src-tauri/src/network.rs): gossipsub + identify + inbound TCP
- Overlay commands: `start_network`, `subscribe_conversation`, `publish_signaling`, `is_overlay_peer_connected`, `dial_contact`
- Asymmetric `derive_session_key` for pre-Noise signaling encryption
- [`ensureTextTransport`](../src/lib/transport/peer-connection.ts) + [`useAutoConnect`](../src/hooks/use-auto-connect.ts)
- Connect dialog moved to chat overflow menu

## Manual test

1. Establish libp2p connection between two contacts (rendezvous + relay per 0008, or direct dial on LAN)
2. Open chat on both — auto WebRTC without connect links
3. Fallback: Advanced → manual connect link
